What JEE Workbench keeps, and why

What we keep

  • Account: your name, and your email, phone number or Google account id, depending on how you sign in. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Your work: test answers, scores, time per question, bookmarks, mistakes, study plan, syllabus progress, mock-test log, saved colleges, JoSAA choice list and assistant chats.
  • Optional: category, gender, home state and target date, used only by the predictor and the study plan.
  • Reviews and reports you send. Reviews are shown without your name after an admin checks them.
  • Sign-in times and a count of sign-ins, so the admin can see the site is used.

Who sees it

You, and the site's admins. Assistant questions are sent to free AI models (through OpenRouter) to get an answer. Phone sign-in codes go through Tekurious, and Google sign-in through Google. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Students under 18

We ask a parent or guardian by email first. Until they agree, nothing about the student is kept beyond the sign-up itself. If nobody answers within {{ days }} days, or they decline, the account is deleted.
